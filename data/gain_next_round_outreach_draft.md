# GAIN next-round outreach — draft messages

*Two asks in one campaign: (1) invite NEW examples for the next round, and (2) ask past
submitters to share the report/data behind a PREVIOUSLY reported example so the Secretariat can
compute SDG indicators and feed the other GAIN workstreams. Personalise the bracketed fields.*

---

## A. To a previous example submitter (data/report request)

**Subject:** GAIN next round — your [YEAR] example, and a small data ask

Dear [Name / focal point],

As we prepare for the **next round of GAIN**, we'd first love to **hear about any new examples**
of including forcibly displaced people in your statistics since you last reported — new surveys,
census outputs, administrative data, or analyses.

We're also reaching out about the example you already shared — **[example title, YEAR]**. To make
these examples go further, the Secretariat is now **computing comparable SDG indicators** for
refugees / IDPs / stateless people (water, sanitation, electricity, school attendance, birth
registration, poverty, food security) directly from the underlying data, and presenting them on a
shared map and in the indicator library.

Would you be able to **share the report or the (anonymised) microdata** behind that example? Even
a single cross-tabulation of the standard indicators by displacement status is enough. This lets
us:
- place your data on the **GAIN SDG map** (comparable, attributed to your office),
- populate the **indicator library** and other GAIN workstreams,
- and show a worked example others can follow.

We handle the computation and share results back for your review before anything is published, and
we follow your data-sharing terms. Happy to send our indicator definitions (JMP ladders, ILO, SDG
metadata) so you can see exactly what we'd derive.

With thanks for your continued leadership in inclusive statistics,

[Name] — EGRISS Secretariat / GAIN

---

## B. Short version (for a broad mailing / newsletter)

We're preparing the **next round of GAIN** and want to hear about your **new examples** of
including refugees, IDPs and stateless people in official statistics. We're also inviting past
contributors to **share the report or data** behind an example already submitted — the Secretariat
can then **compute SDG indicators** from it for the shared map and indicator library, fully
attributed and shared back for your review. Reply to add an example or to discuss data sharing.

---

## Notes for the sender
- Prioritise **country-led (government) examples** — 257 of the 413 in GAIN — and the **2021+
  data** window (data year, not report year; pre-2018 data stays out of the SDG map).
- Pair each request with the map link so submitters can see what their data becomes.
- Contacts and per-office details: `gain_reachout_list.csv` + `contacts_*.rds`. Census-specific
  custom-tabulation asks use `census_sdg_data_request_template.md`.
