In support of the Sustainable Development Goals
Values are computed from microdata, extracted from official reports, or estimated (labelled per card). Scope: GAIN examples · government-produced where possible · data year 2018+ (post-IRRS). A ◆ marks an EGRISS priority indicator; a ✻ marks a value from a GAIN example. Built by the EGRISS Secretariat · GAIN. Not for redistribution of underlying micro-level data.
